Frequently asked questions

How do I clear the example lists?

The app opens with example lists so you can try it. Tap Clear examples on the Home or History screen to remove them and start your own.

How does family sharing work?

Go to Settings › Family sharing › Start a family list and send the link to your family. Everyone who joins sees the same lists and history and can add or tick items. Each person needs Purchase on their iPhone and must be signed in to iCloud.

Where is my data stored?

Your lists and history are saved on your iPhone. If you use a family list, they are shared through your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

Use Settings › Your data › Back up everything to save one file with your lists, settings and history. On the new iPhone, use Restore a backup. Restoring replaces what is currently in the app.

Can I export my purchases?

Yes. Settings › Your data › Export purchases creates a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

Purchase Premium and subscriptions

Purchase is free to download and includes a 2-month free trial. After the trial, a Purchase Premium subscription ($19.99 a year, or the equivalent in your currency) is needed to keep using the app. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the trial ends. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To manage or cancel it, open Settings › Purchase Premium › Manage in the app, or the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Tap Clear examples or delete items and lists in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone. To remove a shared family list, leave or stop sharing it from Settings. Shared data can also be removed in the iPhone Settings app › your name › iCloud.
