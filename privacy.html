Privacy Policy

Purchase: Family Shopping List · Last updated 23 October 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy
In short: Purchase does not collect your personal data. Your lists and purchase history stay on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud if you share a family list. We have no servers that store your data, and we use no advertising or analytics.

Who we are

Purchase is developed by Amit Kumar ("we", "us"). Contact: amitchristile@gmail.com.

Information the app stores

The app stores what you enter: lists, items, quantities, notes such as shop names, prices, shopping trips and appliance service or warranty dates. All of this is stored on your device.

Family sharing and iCloud

If you start or join a family list, that list and its history are stored in Apple's iCloud using CloudKit sharing, under your own iCloud account. Only the people you invite can see it. We cannot see this data. Apple handles iCloud under its own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchase Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Backups and exports

When you export a CSV file or create a backup, the file is saved to the place you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive, or shared using the iOS share sheet. You control where these files go.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders such as appliance service or warranty dates are scheduled on your device. No data is sent to us.

Deleting your data

You can delete items and lists in the app, or delete the app to remove all data stored on your iPhone. You can remove shared family-list data by leaving or stopping sharing in the app, or through Settings › your name › iCloud.

Children

Purchase is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
